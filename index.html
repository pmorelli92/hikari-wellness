What is Reiki

A gentle practice of balance

Reiki is a Japanese healing tradition that works with the body's natural energy. In a session, you rest fully clothed while light, still touch helps calm the nervous system, ease stress, and invite a deep sense of relaxation. Hikari means light — the intention behind every session is to help you reconnect with your own.
